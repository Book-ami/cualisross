Investigating Abilene Christian University’s Ask-A-Librarian Platform: Springshare LibAnswers

Part I. Description of Brown Library & SpringShare LibAnswers

When determining an organization whose information system I would investigate and make suggestions to improve, I decided to choose the one I am most familiar with. From January 2024 to May 2025, I worked as a circulation desk assistant at Margarett & Herman Brown Library. The library of this private university not only serves the students and faculty of Abilene Christian University, but also the local community.

Abilene Christian University is a part of the Abilene Library Consortium which includes Abilene Public Library, other local universities, and cultural heritage organizations (Abilene Library Consortium 2021). Brown Library’s mission clearly stated on its homepage:

“Brown Library serves students, faculty and visiting scholars as the central place for collaboration and study with assistance from reference librarians, writing center staff and technology specialists…offering access to millions of books and journals that further teaching and learning from anywhere."

- echoes its motto “Beyond Books”.

Also present on the homepage is a bar of icons starting with Ask-A-Librarian.

Background

Ask-A-Librarian is a virtual reference services application through which users can contact librarians with reference requests or other services issues. After clicking on the Ask-A-Librarian icon, users can visit the related web page that lists contact information including email, chat, phone and X (formerly known as Twitter) as well as form to submit detailed information on the issue. When one scrolls to the bottom of the page, it is apparent that Ask-A-Librarian is powered by Springshare.

What is SpringShare?

Springshare is a cloud software company geared towards libraries and other educational institutions for almost 20 years. Their mission is to “help libraries provide great service to users and enhance their online services.” (Springshare)

The SpringShare software that Brown Library utilizes is LibApps. When clicking on the hyperlink to log in to LibApps. The web page itself explains what LibApps is.

LibAnswers is the Springshare tool powering Ask-A-Librarian. Ask-a-Librarian complements in person reference services. Its usefulness lies in that the physical reference desk can not always be manned. As a circulation desk assistant, I observed that LibAnswers was used to receive and answer some questions remotely, but its main use was to log in-person questions rather than virtual reference services.

In the investigation of LibAnswers, there are two primary objectives. While my objective to learn and become more familiar with the with platform behind Ask-a-Librarian: Springshare LibApps, my objective for my client, ACU Brown Library is to increase usability of Ask-a-Librarian feature for library Patrons.

Part 2. Evaluating LibAnswers (Ask-a-Librarian)

I decided to evaluate LibAnswers using the ten Nielsen Heuristic Criteria and determined that there were 3 areas for improvement. Based on the first heuristic (visibility of system status, it is unclear whether live virtual help is available for the Ask-a-Librarian feature. Patrons receive email confirmation while waiting for response unable to see status. Based on the sixth heuristic criterion (Recognition rather than recall), a pop-up description or a widget would remind users that the Ask-a-Librarian feature is available but instead it blends in with the other icons. Finally, based on Nielsen’s tenth heuristic criterion for evaluation (help and documentation), I observed that there is no help section. At bottom of Ask- A- Librarian page, the link to Springshare does not lead to the page’s help section and link to Report a Technical Issue redirects to same page.

Part 3: Critical Analysis of LibAnswers and Recommendations

Analysis

While evaluating LibAnswers as the software tool that powered Ask-A-Librarian. There were a few highlights of note. An observation that I made was that the knowledgebase had not been updated since 2022 and many of the 81 answers were older. I learned that Springshare was a software company that has been making software innovation in libraries. LibAnswers as software has good functionality but may be underutilized by library patrons.

Recommendations

There are two recommendations to consider for improving the functionality of LibAnswers. The first would be to add the LibChat widget. The pop up chat interface of a LibChat widget would make the availability of virtual reference services more apparent. Secondly, questions and answers within the knowledge could be updated more frequently for a more robust knowledge base.

Conclusion

LibAnswers functions adequately as the tool behind Ask-A-Librarian. However, to improve usability LibChat should be implemented.
